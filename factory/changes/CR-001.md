audit required by ENG-106 AC14?**

Two resolutions, both of which clear the escalation without touching the product
diff:

- **Option A — approve the substitution.** Record a Founder Decision at
  `factory/changes/CR-001.decision.md` (written by a human, never by this pack)

Either resolution approves cleanly. The product change itself is not disputed:
the approver's assessment is that "the work is good" and it "would approve it on
its own merits".
