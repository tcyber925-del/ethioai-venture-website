## Discovered conflict

*The required **Conflict** field — what actually conflicted, as an observation
rather than a conclusion.*

In both jobs the spec wrote its Impeccable criterion as founder-owned and
blocked. The decision that would justify treating it as satisfied exists only as
conversation, not as a record in the repository or the tracker.

No source file conflicts here, and neither product diff is in question — both
approvers state they would approve on the merits. The conflict is between
`build.md` and every durable record of the criterion.

`002`'s approver:

> **Approving would launder an unrecorded founder decision into an approved PR.**

`003`'s approver:

> acceptance criterion 16 is founder-owned, and the record claims it is satisfied
> on a founder decision that does not exist in any durable form.

## Why implementation could not continue safely

Both jobs were at `needs_human` with the criterion escalated, and the pack's own
rule is that an escalated criterion cannot be marked satisfied by the party that
raised it. Each job's `build.md` recorded the criterion as blocked, so neither
could reach a passing gate on its own evidence. Continuing without a recorded
decision would have meant either asserting a criterion that was never checked,
or shipping a PR whose own record contradicts the approval that let it through.
