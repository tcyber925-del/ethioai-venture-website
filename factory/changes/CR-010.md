## Source facts

*Quoted, not summarised.*

`SKILL.md:659` (`/factory rework`, step 2) permits a human-requested reset:

> Confirm the budget is exhausted **or a human explicitly requests the reset.**
> If the job is not at `needs_human` with an exhausted budget, report its stage
> and stop.

`tools/transition.mjs:531-537` implements only the first branch:

> The retry budget is reset by ONE thing: a human rework, and nothing else.
> `/factory rework` says the job must be "at `needs_human` with an exhausted
> budget", and until that is machine-checked the reset is a promise rather than
> a rule — a writer that kept handing the budget back could spin the loop for
> ever, one reset at a time.

The guard at `:548-555` (`REWORK_BUDGET_LEFT`) refuses any write touching
`retries_left` or `retry_budget_reset_at` while `retries_left` is not 0.

`SKILL.md` (`/factory go`, preconditions) requires that stamp to resume:

> Job at `awaiting_plan_approval` with `spec.md` read by a human, **or** at
> `needs_human` with a recorded rework (`retry_budget_reset_at` set and
> `retries_left` above zero).

`reference/state-machine.md:15-18` describes the one exit:

> `needs_human` is a **pause**, not a terminal state. It has one exit, taken
> only after a human rework re-grants the budget: `/factory rework` stamps
> `retry_budget_reset_at`, and `/factory go` then resumes the job through the
> `needs_human` → `repairing` row.

`reference/recovery.md:32-34` names resuming the designed path:

> Resuming is a separate, human-gated act. `recover` reports what would come
> next; a human then runs `/factory go <job-id>`, which carries the resume
> guarantees.

**Consequence (observation).** The documented human-requested reset branch is
unreachable: `/factory go` requires the rework stamp, and the rework stamp is
writable only when the budget is exhausted. Every `needs_human` park that is
not budget exhaustion — a dead spec-writer or reviewer stage, a
`STATUS: BLOCKED` build, a `blocked` or `not_run` check — has no exit under the
current writer. This is the complement of CR-004 (resolved by ENG-114, PR #44):
that CR gave an exit to the exhausted-budget park; this one concerns the
remaining-budget park.

## Decision needed

Choose how a `needs_human` park with budget remaining resumes:

- **Option A — machine-check the prose's second branch.** Permit the rework
  stamp when the job is at `needs_human` and the human explicitly requests the
  reset (for example: `rework` gains an explicit human-request marker, or
  requires a `--cause` naming the request), without raising `retries_left`
  above what remains. Smallest change to the writer; resolves the SKILL.md /
  tool divergence for every park class; keeps the anti-spin property, since
  `/factory go` still spends a retry through the existing
  `needs_human` → `repairing` row.
- **Option B — add a `needs_human` → `reviewing` resume row.** For an
  interrupted review round specifically: verdicts partially present, none
  carrying a blocking finding, budget remaining, resumed only by
  `/factory go`. Matches this job's shape, but adds a second exit to
  `needs_human` and does not help other park classes.
- **Option C — keep the strict rule; declare remaining-budget parks terminal.**
  Document that a `needs_human` park with `retries_left > 0` can only be
  superseded or abandoned, and align `SKILL.md:659` by deleting the
  "or a human explicitly requests the reset" branch.

## Inference (labelled — not a source fact)

Option A is the smallest change that makes the documented command work as
written and covers every park class. Option B fits this job's exact shape.
Option C is the honest documentation of today's behaviour. Whatever is chosen
lands as its own pack-update job on `main`; this factory run does not edit the
pack.
