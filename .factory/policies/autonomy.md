# Autonomy Policy

## AUTO
Bounded changes that do not alter product direction or architecture.

## REVIEW
Approved product/UX work; human review is required before merge.

## APPROVAL
Architecture, infrastructure, security, authentication, paid services, major public claims, or V1 scope changes. Prepare context/plan and wait for explicit approval.

## Repair loop
Automatic CI repair may run at most twice. If verification still fails, block the work and request human intervention.
