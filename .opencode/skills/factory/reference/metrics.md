# Baseline metrics — collected descriptively

Collected descriptively. **No numerical targets before a baseline exists.**

- **Intake clarification rate** — how often intake needs a follow-up.
- **Completion, blocked, and rejection rates** — terminal outcomes.
- **Verification failure rate and categories** — check failures by reason.
- **Review findings per job and repeat categories** — recurrence signal.
- **Average repair rounds** — rework cost.
- **Intake to evidence-ready time** — cycle latency.
- **Time awaiting human review** — human-gate latency.
- **Merge approval rate** — approvals versus rejections.
- **Post-merge regression or revert rate where observable** — observed outcome.
- **Percentage of jobs with complete evidence** — evidence discipline.
- **Percentage of jobs with a recorded human decision** — governance discipline.
